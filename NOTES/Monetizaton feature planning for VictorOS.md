- Expiration date

# Expired Subscription

When a subscription expires

VictorOS does **not** stop working.

Instead it gracefully returns to the Free plan.

Users keep

- Their files
- Their conversations
- Their local memories
- Their settings

Premium features simply become unavailable until the subscription is renewed.
